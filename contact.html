Good questions

Is there a minimum order?

Bath powders (Nalangu Maavu, Baby and Men's) have a minimum of 250 g. There's no minimum on other products.

How do I pay?

Once we confirm your order on WhatsApp, we share UPI and bank details. No card details are ever collected on this website.

Do you deliver outside my city?

Yes, we ship across India. Delivery charges depend on weight and pincode, and we tell you the exact amount before you pay.

How long do the powders last?

Because there are no preservatives, store them airtight in a dry place and always use a dry spoon. Never take powder from the pack with wet hands. Stored this way they keep well for months.

Can men use Nalangu Maavu?

We make a separate Bath Powder for Men without kasthuri manjal, since kasthuri manjal is traditionally used to reduce facial hair over time.

Is the baby bath powder safe for newborns?

It contains only almond, green gram and chana dal, with no turmeric or fragrance. As with anything new, do a small patch test first and consult your paediatrician if your baby has a skin condition.

Does the eeyachombu contain lead?

No. The coating is vellieeyam (pure tin), which is a metal by itself and does not contain lead. It's heavy because it's solid metal, hand-hammered by artisans in Kumbakonam.

How do I use an eeyachombu safely?
  • Fill it with water or puli thanni before switching on the stove. Never heat it empty, as tin melts at a low temperature.
  • Cook only on low to medium heat.
  • Lift with a thick kitchen cloth. Don't use tongs or an idukki.
Who should not take the Vendhayam Omam Karunjeeragam podi?

It is not advisable for pregnant or breastfeeding women, or women trying to conceive. If you're on medication for diabetes or blood pressure, speak to your doctor before taking it regularly.
